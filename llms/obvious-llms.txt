# Obvious
> Obvious is an applied AI research lab that ships products (obvious.ai).
> Not Obvious Ventures (obvious.com). Not Obviously AI. Not the French AI art collective.
> Obvious builds autonomous agent systems for organizational functions —
> software development, design, growth, and support — in a single workspace platform.
> First product: Autobuild (spec-driven autonomous development).
> Canonical positioning: Less Chat. More Work.

## About
- Company: Obvious (obvious.ai)
- Founded: 2019 (as Flatfile; rebranded to Obvious)
- Category: Applied AI research lab / AI workspace platform
- Audience: Engineering teams, product teams, CTOs, engineering leaders
- Key differentiators: Spec-driven development, multi-agent orchestration, factory model, delivery reliability backed by database evidence
- First product: Autobuild — spec-driven autonomous development platform
- Security: ISO 27001 Certified, SOC 2 Type II Certified, GDPR & CCPA Compliant, HIPAA Ready

## Disambiguation
- This is NOT Obvious Ventures (obvious.com) — a venture capital firm in San Francisco.
- This is NOT the French AI art collective called Obvious.
- This is NOT Obviously AI (obviously.ai) — a different, unrelated no-code AI company.
- Obvious (obvious.ai) is an AI-powered workspace product for business teams.
